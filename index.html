Four ways to compete, one league. Here's how each one works.
View Live Standings →

🏆 FPL Fairplay League Classic

The main event — season-long, no eliminations
  • Every manager's total FPL points are added up across all 38 gameweeks.
  • Standings update automatically after each gameweek is finalized by the official FPL scoring.
  • Whoever has the highest cumulative total at the end of GW38 wins the league.
  • No knockouts here — a bad gameweek doesn't eliminate you, it's a pure marathon.

⚔️ Head to Head H2H

Weekly 1v1 matchups, football-style table
  • Every gameweek, you're paired against one other manager in the league.
  • Higher FPL score that gameweek wins the match: 3 points for a win, 1 point for a tie (equal scores), 0 for a loss.
  • Standings rank by total H2H league points (win/draw/loss record), with total FPL points scored as the tiebreaker.
  • Fixtures rotate automatically for the whole season, similar to a round-robin football league.

🥇 FPL Cup Knockout

Single-elimination bracket, later in the season
  • FPL automatically activates the cup for classic leagues after a qualification cutoff (typically once a decent number of gameweeks have been played).
  • Managers are randomly paired each round; whoever scores more points that gameweek advances.
  • Ties are broken by FPL's standard tiebreak rules (bench points, then transfers made).
  • Continues round by round until one manager remains as Cup champion.
Assumption: exact start gameweek and pairing rules are set by FPL itself for this season and aren't final until FPL activates them — the live standings page will show bracket results automatically once that happens.

💀 Last Man Standing Elimination

Survive each gameweek — one bad week and you're out
  • Starts at Gameweek 5 — everyone in the league begins as a survivor.
  • Each gameweek from GW5 onward, the surviving manager with the lowest gameweek score is eliminated.
  • If there's a tie for lowest score, the manager with the lower season total points (as of that gameweek) is the one eliminated.
  • This repeats every week until only one manager is left standing — the winner.

📅 Manager of the Month Monthly

Best performer each month, awarded manually
  • Awarded each calendar month to the manager with the best gameweek performances during that month.
  • Not yet on the live standings page — for now this is tracked and awarded manually by the league admin.
Rules for the Cup are FPL-set and may be refined once the season reaches that stage.
